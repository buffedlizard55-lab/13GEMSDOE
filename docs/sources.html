DOE-funded shallow thermal methods and limits ↗
Richard Zehner; DOE grantEE0002830 (2014)
  • Shallow2m surveys can delineate some steam-heated fault zones; seasonal adjustment used control stations.
  • Solar effects, hard substrates and shallow groundwater can obscure/mislead the thermal signal.
Supports testing a mechanism, not R15 performance or a geological discovery. McGee Mountain itself is outside our competition latitude bounds.2026-10-01
GeoDAWN airborne data ↗
USGS; Glen and Earney (2024)
  • DOI10.5066/P93LGLVQ; CC0-1.0.
  • Radiometry informs surface geology/soil composition; magnetics informs subsurface geology/structure.
  • Survey flight height/line spacing vary; 100m raster sampling is not uniform100m geological information resolution.
Local8-bit derivatives are pinned mirrors, not the original native full-precision grids.2026-10-01

  • Fault tips, stepovers, intersections, accommodation and transfer zones are structural classes.
